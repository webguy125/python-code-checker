How this site handles information

Python Code Checker is designed as a lightweight web utility. We do not ask users to create accounts or submit personal profiles to use the tool.

  • Code pasted into the tool may be sent to the service backend to process the repair and mock-test request.
  • Your browser may store the last pasted input locally on your device for convenience.
  • Basic hosting, analytics, or advertising providers may collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, and page activity according to their own policies.
  • If advertising is enabled, ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads.

If you do not want local browser persistence, clear your browser storage after use or use a private browsing session.
